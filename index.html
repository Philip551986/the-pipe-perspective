What is The Pipe Perspective?

A single publication by theolayiwolapipe, bringing together faith, science, engineering, enterprise and society. Six recurring series organize the writing, while fifteen categories give each subject room to develop.

Do I need a technical background?

No. Reflective essays are written for a broad audience. More technical pieces will explain their context and identify any tools or prior knowledge needed.

Will each series have a separate subscription?

The plan is one newsletter with connected series, so readers can discover ideas across disciplines without joining six separate lists.

How often will new issues arrive?

The launch goal is one substantial edition each week. The final schedule will be confirmed when subscriptions open.

Can I subscribe now?

Subscriptions are not open yet. This page introduces the publication and its themes. The sign-up will be connected to a mailing service before launch.

Where does Micro2Nano fit in?

Micro2Nano Industry Brief is the series focused on connecting university expertise with industrial challenges, consulting ideas, research partnerships and product development.

The opening perspective

At the intersection of faith, engineering and innovation.

Some questions belong in a laboratory. Others begin in a place of faith, a classroom or a conversation about the future. Often, the most useful insights emerge when those conversations meet.

That is the idea behind The Pipe Perspective: a publication that connects how we think, what we build and why it matters. Faith invites reflection on purpose and responsibility. Engineering gives us tools to understand systems and solve problems. Enterprise asks how useful ideas can reach the people who need them.

These connections deserve careful exploration. A technical model can teach us about feedback. A research project can reveal an industrial opportunity. A question about leadership can challenge how we teach and mentor.

Here, you will find practical explanations alongside reflective essays: on modelling and microelectronics, AI and research, faith and personal growth, and the possibilities for industry and society.

The ambition is simple: to turn curiosity into understanding, and understanding into meaningful action. Welcome to the conversation.
